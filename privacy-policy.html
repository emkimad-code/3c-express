Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Learn how 3C Express collects, uses and protects your personal information.

1. Data We Collect

When you submit a quote request or contact us, we may collect:

• Company name
• Contact person name
• Email address
• Phone number
• Shipment information
• Any information voluntarily provided through our forms

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your information is used exclusively for:

• Preparing quotations
• Managing freight operations
• Customer communication
• Customs and logistics coordination
• Improving our services

3. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or to comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

3C Express does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only with:

• Transport partners
• Customs authorities
• Service providers involved in logistics operations
• Legal authorities when required by law

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

6. Your Rights

In accordance with applicable data protection regulations, you may have the right to:

• Access your personal data
• Correct inaccurate information
• Request deletion of your data
• Restrict or object to processing
• Request data portability where applicable

This website may use essential cookies required for proper operation and user experience.
