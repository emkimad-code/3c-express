Packing List Guide

Learn why a Packing List is essential for customs clearance and international shipping, and how to prepare it correctly.

July 2026 • 5 min read

Contents

Introduction What is a Packing List? Information Included Why It Matters Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

A Packing List is one of the most important shipping documents used in international logistics. It provides detailed information about the contents of a shipment and helps customs authorities, carriers and receivers identify every package.

Although it does not replace the Commercial Invoice, it complements it by describing how the goods are packed.

What is a Packing List?

A Packing List is a document that details every package included in a shipment. It describes the quantity, dimensions, weight and packaging method of the goods being transported.

It accompanies international shipments regardless of whether they travel by air, sea or road.

Expert Tip

Always make sure your Packing List matches your Commercial Invoice. Differences between the two documents are one of the most common causes of customs inspections.

Information included in a Packing List

  • Shipper and consignee details
  • Package numbers
  • Number of cartons or pallets
  • Gross and net weight
  • Package dimensions
  • Description of goods
  • Marks and numbers

Why the Packing List matters

The Packing List helps customs officers verify shipments, assists warehouse teams during handling and enables the consignee to check received goods.

It also simplifies inspections and reduces the risk of shipment discrepancies.

Key Takeaways

  • A Packing List describes how goods are packed.
  • It complements the Commercial Invoice.
  • Accurate information speeds up customs clearance.
  • It helps carriers, customs officers and receivers identify cargo.

FAQ

Is a Packing List mandatory?

It is highly recommended and often required for international commercial shipments.

Does the Packing List show prices?

No. Prices are normally shown on the Commercial Invoice, not on the Packing List.

Can 3C Express verify my Packing List?

Yes. Our logistics specialists can review your shipping documents before your goods are dispatched.

Need help preparing your shipping documents?

Our customs specialists can help ensure your Packing List complies with international logistics requirements.
