Container Sizes Guide

Learn the main differences between 20ft, 40ft and High Cube containers to choose the right option for your sea freight shipment.

July 2026 • 5 min read

Contents

Introduction 20ft Container 40ft Container High Cube Container Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

Choosing the right container size is an important part of planning an international sea freight shipment. The most common options are 20ft, 40ft and 40ft High Cube containers.

The best choice depends on cargo volume, weight, dimensions and loading requirements.

20ft container

A 20ft container is commonly used for heavy cargo, smaller volumes and dense goods. It offers less internal volume than a 40ft container but can often handle significant weight.

This option is frequently used for industrial goods, machinery, raw materials and dense palletised cargo.

Expert Tip

A larger container is not always the best choice. Heavy cargo may reach weight limits before filling the available volume.

40ft container

A 40ft container provides more space and is often selected for larger shipments, lighter cargo or higher-volume goods.

It is a common solution for retail products, furniture, cartons, textiles and general cargo.

40ft High Cube container

A 40ft High Cube container offers additional internal height compared with a standard 40ft container. This can be useful for tall cargo or shipments where extra vertical space improves loading efficiency.

High Cube containers are often used when volume matters more than weight.

Key Takeaways

  • 20ft containers are often suitable for heavy or dense cargo.
  • 40ft containers offer more space for larger shipments.
  • High Cube containers provide extra height.
  • The right choice depends on both cargo volume and weight.

FAQ

Which container is best for heavy cargo?

A 20ft container is often suitable for heavy cargo because dense goods may reach weight limits before filling a larger container.

What is the advantage of a High Cube container?

A High Cube container offers extra internal height, which is useful for taller cargo or improved loading capacity.

Can 3C Express help choose the right container?

Yes. 3C Express can help evaluate your cargo dimensions, weight and destination to recommend the most suitable sea freight option.
