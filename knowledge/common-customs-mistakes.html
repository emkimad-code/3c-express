Common Customs Mistakes

Avoid the most frequent customs errors that cause delays, additional costs and shipment disruptions.

July 2026 • 6 min read

Contents

Introduction Incorrect Documents Wrong HS Codes Incorrect Values Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

Customs clearance can be delayed when shipment information is incomplete, inconsistent or incorrect. Even small errors may result in inspections, storage charges or additional communication with customs authorities.

Understanding the most common customs mistakes helps businesses prepare better documents and keep international shipments moving smoothly.

Incorrect or missing documents

One of the most common causes of customs delays is missing or incomplete documentation. Commercial invoices, packing lists and transport documents must be accurate and consistent.

  • Missing Commercial Invoice
  • Incomplete Packing List
  • Incorrect consignee details
  • Missing transport document references

Expert Tip

Before shipping, compare the Commercial Invoice, Packing List and transport document line by line to make sure all details match.

Wrong HS Codes

HS Codes are used to classify products for customs purposes. Incorrect classification may lead to wrong duty calculations, customs questions or delayed clearance.

When in doubt, businesses should verify product classification before export or import.

Incorrect customs values

Customs authorities use declared values to calculate duties and taxes. Incorrect or unrealistic values can trigger inspections and additional checks.

The declared value should reflect the real commercial transaction and match the supporting invoice.

Key Takeaways

  • Incomplete documents are a major cause of customs delays.
  • HS Codes must be accurate.
  • Declared values should match the commercial transaction.
  • Consistent information reduces the risk of inspections.

FAQ

What causes most customs delays?

Most delays are caused by missing documents, incorrect product descriptions, wrong values or inconsistent shipment information.

Can customs mistakes increase costs?

Yes. Delays may lead to storage fees, additional handling charges and extra administrative work.

Can 3C Express help avoid customs mistakes?

Yes. Our customs specialists can review documents and help prepare shipments correctly before departure.

Want to avoid customs delays?

Our team can help review your documents and reduce the risk of customs issues before your shipment moves.
