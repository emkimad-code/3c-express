Incoterms® 2020 Explained

Understand how Incoterms® 2020 define responsibilities, costs and risks between buyers and sellers in international trade.

July 2026 • 8 min read

Contents

Introduction What Are Incoterms? Main Incoterms® 2020 Rules Why They Matter Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

Incoterms® 2020 are internationally recognised trade rules that define the responsibilities of buyers and sellers during international transactions.

They clarify who pays for transport, who handles customs procedures, where risk transfers and which party is responsible at each stage of the shipment.

What are Incoterms?

Incoterms are standardised rules published by the International Chamber of Commerce. They are used in contracts, invoices and shipping documents to avoid misunderstandings between buyers and sellers.

Choosing the right Incoterm is essential because it directly affects transport costs, insurance, customs responsibilities and delivery risk.

Expert Tip

Always confirm the Incoterm before shipping. A single term can completely change who is responsible for freight costs, customs clearance and final delivery.

Main Incoterms® 2020 rules

Some Incoterms are used for all modes of transport, while others are specific to sea freight.

  • EXW: the buyer takes responsibility almost from the seller's premises.
  • FCA: the seller delivers goods to a named carrier or place.
  • FOB: commonly used for sea freight, with risk transferring when goods are loaded on board.
  • CIF: the seller pays cost, insurance and freight to the destination port.
  • DAP: the seller delivers goods to a named destination, excluding import clearance.
  • DDP: the seller handles delivery including import duties and taxes.

Why Incoterms matter

Incoterms help avoid disputes by clearly defining responsibilities. They also help freight forwarders prepare accurate quotations and organise transport according to the agreed terms.

Incorrect or unclear Incoterms can lead to unexpected costs, delays and confusion during customs clearance.

Key Takeaways

  • Incoterms® 2020 define responsibilities between buyer and seller.
  • They affect transport costs, risk transfer and customs duties.
  • Some Incoterms are specific to sea freight.
  • Clear Incoterms help avoid unexpected charges and delays.

FAQ

Are Incoterms mandatory?

They are not mandatory by law in every transaction, but they are strongly recommended because they clarify responsibilities in international trade.

Which Incoterm is best for importers?

The best Incoterm depends on the buyer's experience, preferred level of control and responsibility for transport and customs procedures.
