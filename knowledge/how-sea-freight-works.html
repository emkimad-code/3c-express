How Sea Freight Works

Discover how international sea freight works, from booking your shipment to final delivery at destination.

July 2026 • 7 min read

Contents

Introduction Booking the Shipment Ocean Transport Final Delivery Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

Sea freight is one of the most cost-effective ways to transport goods internationally. Millions of containers move between ports every year, making ocean freight the backbone of global trade.

Understanding each stage of the shipping process helps businesses improve planning, avoid delays and better manage international logistics.

Booking the shipment

The process begins with booking cargo space through a freight forwarder. The shipper provides shipment details including origin, destination, cargo dimensions, weight and preferred departure date.

Once confirmed, transport to the departure port is organised and export documentation is prepared.

Expert Tip

Booking several weeks before departure generally provides more sailing options and competitive freight rates.

Ocean transport

After customs export clearance, containers are loaded onto the vessel. During the voyage, shipments can be tracked while crossing international shipping routes.

Transit times vary depending on the ports involved, vessel schedules and possible transhipments.

Arrival and final delivery

Once the vessel arrives, containers are unloaded, customs import procedures are completed and cargo is released for final delivery.

Depending on the service selected, delivery may be arranged directly to the consignee's warehouse or business premises.

Key Takeaways

  • Sea freight begins with shipment booking.
  • Export customs clearance is completed before departure.
  • Ocean transport follows scheduled vessel routes.
  • Import customs clearance takes place before final delivery.

FAQ

How long does sea freight usually take?

Transit times depend on the origin, destination and shipping route. International shipments generally take several weeks.

Can shipments be tracked during transport?

Yes. Most ocean freight shipments can be tracked throughout the transportation process.

Can 3C Express organise the complete shipping process?

Yes. 3C Express manages booking, documentation, customs clearance and final delivery for international sea freight shipments.
