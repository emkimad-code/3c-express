Full Truck Load vs Groupage

Understand the differences between dedicated truck transport and groupage solutions to choose the right road freight option.

July 2026 • 5 min read

Contents

Introduction Full Truck Load Groupage Key Differences Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

Road freight offers different transport solutions depending on shipment size, urgency and budget. Two of the most common options are Full Truck Load and groupage transport.

Understanding the difference helps businesses optimise costs while maintaining reliable delivery performance.

What is Full Truck Load?

Full Truck Load, often called FTL, means the entire truck is reserved for one shipment or one customer. It is usually selected for larger shipments, urgent deliveries or goods requiring direct transport.

  • Dedicated truck
  • Direct delivery
  • Reduced handling
  • Suitable for large or urgent shipments

Expert Tip

FTL is often the best option when speed, security and reduced handling are more important than sharing transport costs.

What is groupage?

Groupage allows several shipments from different customers to be consolidated in the same truck. Each customer pays only for the space used by their cargo.

This solution is ideal for smaller shipments that do not require a dedicated vehicle.

FTL vs groupage: key differences

Both solutions are useful depending on cargo volume, budget and delivery expectations.

  • Cost: groupage is usually more economical for smaller shipments.
  • Speed: FTL is often faster because the truck goes directly to destination.
  • Handling: FTL reduces handling compared with consolidated transport.
  • Flexibility: groupage is practical for regular small shipments.

Key Takeaways

  • FTL reserves a full truck for one shipment.
  • Groupage shares truck space with other shipments.
  • FTL is better for urgent or high-volume cargo.
  • Groupage is cost-effective for smaller shipments.

FAQ

Which option is cheaper?

Groupage is usually cheaper for small shipments, while FTL becomes more efficient for larger volumes or urgent deliveries.

Is FTL faster than groupage?

Yes. FTL usually provides faster transit because the truck is dedicated to one shipment.

Can 3C Express organise both FTL and groupage?

Yes. 3C Express can organise dedicated truck transport and groupage solutions across Europe.
