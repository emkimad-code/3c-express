Sea Freight Transit Times

Discover the average sea freight transit times between major international ports and the factors that influence delivery schedules.

July 2026 • 5 min read

Contents

Introduction Average Transit Times What Influences Transit Time? Planning Your Shipment Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

Sea freight is one of the most economical methods of transporting goods internationally. However, transit times vary depending on shipping routes, ports, vessel schedules and customs procedures.

Understanding average transit times allows businesses to better plan inventory, production and customer deliveries.

Average transit times

Transit times differ depending on origin and destination. Shipments between Asia and Europe generally take several weeks, while regional European routes are significantly shorter.

  • China → France
  • India → France
  • USA → France
  • Middle East → France
  • Europe → Europe

Expert Tip

Always include a buffer in your supply chain planning. Weather conditions, port congestion and customs inspections can occasionally extend transit times.

What influences transit time?

Several factors can affect the duration of a sea freight shipment, including vessel schedules, transhipments, port congestion, customs inspections and adverse weather conditions.

Working with an experienced freight forwarder helps minimise unnecessary delays and improve shipment visibility.

Planning your shipment

Businesses should plan imports well in advance, especially during peak shipping seasons. Early bookings provide more sailing options and often better freight rates.

Good planning also helps avoid expensive emergency air freight shipments.

Key Takeaways

  • Transit times vary depending on shipping routes.
  • Port congestion and customs can affect schedules.
  • Early planning improves reliability.
  • Freight forwarders help optimise international logistics.

FAQ

How accurate are estimated transit times?

Shipping schedules provide estimated arrival dates, but actual transit times may vary due to operational conditions.

Can I track my sea freight shipment?

Yes. Most international sea freight shipments can be tracked throughout their journey.
