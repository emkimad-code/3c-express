Commercial Invoice Guide

Learn how to prepare a commercial invoice correctly and why it is one of the most important documents for international shipping.

July 2026 • 5 min read

Contents

Introduction What is a Commercial Invoice? Required Information Common Mistakes Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

The Commercial Invoice is one of the most important documents in international trade. Customs authorities use it to determine the value of goods, calculate duties and taxes, and verify shipment details.

An incomplete or inaccurate invoice may result in customs delays, additional inspections or unexpected charges.

What is a Commercial Invoice?

A Commercial Invoice is a document issued by the seller describing the goods being exported or imported. It provides customs authorities with the information required to process international shipments.

Unlike a Proforma Invoice, the Commercial Invoice represents the final commercial transaction between buyer and seller.

Expert Tip

Always ensure that the Commercial Invoice matches the Packing List and transport documents. Consistency between documents helps avoid customs delays.

Information that must appear

  • Seller and buyer details
  • Invoice number and date
  • Detailed description of the goods
  • HS Codes when applicable
  • Country of origin
  • Quantity and unit value
  • Total invoice value
  • Currency used
  • Selected Incoterm®

Common mistakes

The most frequent errors include vague product descriptions, incorrect values, missing HS Codes, inconsistent quantities and incomplete seller or buyer information.

Reviewing the invoice before shipment greatly reduces the risk of customs delays.

Key Takeaways

  • The Commercial Invoice is essential for customs clearance.
  • Accurate information speeds up border procedures.
  • It must match all other shipping documents.
  • Errors may result in customs delays and additional costs.

FAQ

Who prepares the Commercial Invoice?

The seller or exporter is normally responsible for preparing the Commercial Invoice.

Is the Commercial Invoice required for every shipment?

It is generally required for international commercial shipments and customs procedures.

Can 3C Express review shipping documents?

Yes. Our logistics specialists can verify your Commercial Invoice and other shipping documents before export.

Need help preparing your shipping documents?

Our customs specialists can help ensure your Commercial Invoice complies with international trade requirements.
