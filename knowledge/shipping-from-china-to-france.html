Shipping from China to France

Understand the key steps, documents and logistics options involved when importing goods from China to France by sea freight.

July 2026 • 8 min read

Contents

Introduction Shipping Process Required Documents Customs Clearance Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

China is one of the main sourcing countries for European importers. Sea freight is often the preferred solution for moving goods from China to France because it offers competitive pricing for larger volumes.

A successful shipment requires proper planning, accurate documentation and reliable coordination between suppliers, freight forwarders and customs teams.

Shipping process from China to France

The process usually starts with cargo collection from the supplier, export customs clearance in China, ocean transport, import customs clearance in France and final delivery to the consignee.

Depending on shipment volume, importers can choose between FCL and LCL sea freight solutions.

Expert Tip

Before confirming an order, always clarify the Incoterm with your supplier. It determines who is responsible for transport, customs formalities and costs at each stage.

Required documents

Importing goods from China to France requires accurate commercial and transport documents. Missing or inconsistent paperwork may delay customs clearance.

  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading
  • HS Code and product description
  • Certificate of Origin if required

Customs clearance in France

Once the cargo arrives in France, customs clearance must be completed before the goods can be released. Duties, VAT and import requirements depend on the HS code, origin, value and nature of the goods.

Working with a freight forwarder helps ensure that documents are complete and import formalities are handled correctly.

Key Takeaways

  • Sea freight is commonly used for importing goods from China to France.
  • FCL and LCL options depend on shipment volume.
  • Accurate documents are essential for customs clearance.
  • Incoterms define responsibilities between buyer and seller.

FAQ

How long does shipping from China to France take?

Sea freight transit times vary depending on the port of origin, destination port, vessel schedule and possible transhipments.

Do I need a customs broker in France?

Import customs clearance is required before goods can be released. A freight forwarder can assist with customs procedures and documentation.

Can 3C Express handle imports from China?

Yes. 3C Express can coordinate sea freight, customs clearance and final delivery for shipments imported from China to France.
