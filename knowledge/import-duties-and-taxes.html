Import Duties & Taxes

Understand how customs duties, import taxes and VAT affect international shipments and how businesses can prepare for these costs.

July 2026 • 7 min read

Contents

Introduction What Are Import Duties? Import VAT How Costs Are Calculated Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

When goods enter a country, they may be subject to customs duties, import taxes and VAT. These charges are collected by customs authorities before goods can be released for delivery.

Understanding these costs helps businesses estimate the true cost of importing goods and avoid unexpected expenses.

What are import duties?

Import duties are customs charges applied to imported goods. The duty rate depends on the product classification, country of origin and applicable trade agreements.

Many countries use the Harmonized System (HS) to classify products and determine duty rates.

Expert Tip

Correctly classifying goods with the appropriate HS Code is one of the best ways to avoid customs disputes and incorrect duty calculations.

Import VAT

In many countries, imported goods are also subject to Value Added Tax (VAT). VAT is generally calculated on the customs value of the goods plus applicable duties and transport costs.

The exact rules depend on the importing country's legislation.

How import costs are calculated

Several factors influence the total amount payable during customs clearance.

  • Customs value of the goods
  • HS Code classification
  • Country of origin
  • Applicable customs duties
  • Import VAT
  • Other regulatory charges if applicable

Key Takeaways

  • Import duties vary depending on product classification.
  • VAT is often payable on imported goods.
  • HS Codes influence customs duty rates.
  • Preparing accurate documentation reduces customs issues.

FAQ

Who pays import duties?

The responsible party depends on the agreed Incoterm® and the commercial agreement between buyer and seller.

Can customs duties be avoided?

Duty exemptions or preferential rates may apply under certain trade agreements, depending on the country and product.

Can 3C Express assist with customs calculations?

Yes. Our customs specialists can help customers understand import procedures and prepare the required documentation.

Need assistance with customs procedures?

Our logistics specialists can help you prepare international shipments and understand customs duties and import taxes.
