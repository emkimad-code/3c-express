7 Ways to Reduce Air Freight Costs

Discover practical strategies to optimise your air freight budget without compromising delivery performance or shipment reliability.

July 2026 • 5 min read

Contents

Introduction 7 Cost Saving Tips Planning Shipments Working with a Freight Forwarder Key Takeaways FAQ

Introduction

Air freight offers unmatched speed for international shipments, but it also represents one of the highest transport costs. Fortunately, businesses can significantly reduce expenses through better planning and smarter logistics decisions.

This guide presents seven practical ways to optimise your air freight budget while maintaining reliable delivery performance.

7 Cost Saving Tips

  1. Plan shipments in advance whenever possible.
  2. Reduce package dimensions to lower volumetric weight.
  3. Consolidate multiple shipments into one shipment.
  4. Select the appropriate service level.
  5. Provide complete shipping documentation.
  6. Avoid unnecessary urgent shipments.
  7. Work with an experienced freight forwarder.

Expert Tip

Reducing package volume is often one of the easiest ways to lower air freight costs because pricing is frequently based on chargeable weight rather than actual weight.

Planning shipments efficiently

Good planning reduces emergency shipments, improves carrier availability and allows businesses to choose more competitive freight solutions.

Forecasting shipping needs also makes cargo consolidation easier and helps avoid premium rates during peak seasons.

Why work with a freight forwarder?

Experienced freight forwarders compare airlines, optimise routing, prepare documentation and recommend the most suitable transport solutions for each shipment.

Professional logistics support often results in lower costs, fewer delays and improved supply chain efficiency.

Key Takeaways

  • Plan shipments as early as possible.
  • Reduce package dimensions whenever possible.
  • Consolidate cargo to improve efficiency.
  • Choose the right logistics partner.

FAQ

Can air freight costs be negotiated?

Yes. Freight rates vary depending on shipment volume, destination, service level and market conditions.

Does packaging affect shipping costs?

Yes. Larger packages increase volumetric weight and therefore the chargeable weight used for pricing.

Can 3C Express help optimise transport costs?
